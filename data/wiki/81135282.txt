'''''No! YOU'RE WRONG. or: Spooky Action at a Distance''''' is a 2025 American film written, edited, produced and directed by [[Crispin Glover]] and starring Glover and his father, [[Bruce Glover]], who also co-wrote the screenplay.

== Synopsis ==
The Muldoon family's wealth and power fuel a cycle of obsession and strife, with father and son locked in a battle for control at its core.

== Production ==
Glover started developing the screenplay in 2007, as a project for him and his father, actor [[Bruce Glover]], to act in together. Production of the film started in 2013 and originally wrapped in 2015, before Glover shot additional production segments yearly thereafter, wrapping principal photography in 2018.<ref name=VanityFair>{{Cite web|url=https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2015/10/everything-is-fine-with-crispin-glover|title = Everything is Fine with Crispin Glover|website = [[Vanity Fair (magazine)|Vanity Fair]]|date = October 2, 2015}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://deepestdream.com/crispin-glover-reflects-on-acting-path-and-lucky-day-collaboration-with-roger-avary/|title=Crispin Glover Reflects On Acting Path And 'Lucky Day' Collaboration With Roger Avary|website=DeepestDream.com|date = October 10, 2019|access-date=September 20, 2025}}</ref> The film was primarily shot on sets constructed on shooting stages within the former horse stables on his property in the [[Czech Republic]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.architecturaldigest.com/story/crispin-glover-czech-castle|title=Inside Crispin Glover's House in the Czech Republic|website = [[Architectural Digest]]|date=June 28, 2017}}</ref>

It was the first of Glover's films to be shot on [[35mm movie film|35mm film stock]], having previously only shot his films on [[16mm film]].<ref name="VanityFair" /> To create an aesthetic comparable to films of the [[silent film era|silent era]], a [[Hand-held camera|hand-cranked camera]] previously owned by [[Karel Zeman]] was used for the segments of the film set in 1868 and 1888.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.spreaker.com/podcast/the-projection-booth-podcast--5513239 |title=Special Report: No! YOU’RE WRONG. (2025)|website = [[The Projection Booth]]|date=October 7, 2025}}</ref>

== Release ==
The film premiered at the [[Museum of Modern Art]] on October 2, 2025.<ref>[https://www.instagram.com/p/DO0BuW2jN_-/]</ref> Like his two previous films, Glover tours with the film accompanied with his live show, a question-and-answer session, and a meet-and-greet/book signing.
